# Agent: houston

## Scope

Open civic data analysis for Houston, Texas — the nation's fourth-largest city.
Covers financial transparency, public safety, 311 service requests, geospatial
infrastructure, and urban environmental monitoring.

## Capabilities

- **Financial Transparency**: Budget, payroll, and checkbook analysis via Open Finance suite
- **Public Safety Analytics**: HPD crime statistics, use-of-force dashboards, traffic stops
- **311 Service Requests**: Hotspot mapping, SLA performance benchmarking (4.5M+ records)
- **Geospatial Infrastructure**: COHGIS layers (parcels, zoning, utilities, floodplains)
- **Environmental Monitoring**: Flood risk, coastal vulnerability, air quality

## Data Sources

- City of Houston Open Data Portal (`data.houstontx.gov`)
- COHGIS Data Hub (150+ GIS feature layers)
- Houston Public Works GIS
- Kinder Institute Urban Data Platform (Rice University)
- UnderstandingHouston.org
