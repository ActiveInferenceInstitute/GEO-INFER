# Symbols and Glossary {#sec:symbols_glossary}

## GEO-INFER

The repository-wide framework measured by the manuscript generator.

## Active Inference

The inference and decision-making methods implemented and tested in
`GEO-INFER-ACT`.

## Bayesian inference

Probabilistic inference methods implemented and tested in `GEO-INFER-BAYES`.

## RISK

Risk, hazard, uncertainty, and resource-lifecycle methods implemented and
tested in `GEO-INFER-RISK`.

## Evidence surface

A tracked source, test, validator, generated artifact, or resolved reference
that can be inspected or regenerated by an explicit command.

## Source fingerprint

The short SHA-256 digest `{{RESEARCH_SOURCE_HASH}}` computed from the tracked
manuscript and module Python source surfaces for this build.

Project: `GEO-INFER`
