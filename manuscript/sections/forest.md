## GEO-INFER-FOREST — Forest Management and Wildfire Risk

GEO-INFER-FOREST provides forest management, carbon sequestration, wildfire risk, and forest ecosystem analysis (module README). It is a Spatial & Place-based theme module, grounding the framework's ecological claims in one of the most geographically explicit domains: standing biomass, canopy structure, and fire danger are all functions of place and time.

The `geo_infer_forest` package exports eleven public symbols from `src/geo_infer_forest/__init__.py`, spanning four concerns. Inventory and health: `ForestInventory` and `ForestHealthMonitor` track stock and condition, with `CanopyAnalyzer` for structural analysis and `DeforestationDetector` for change detection. Carbon: `CarbonSequestrationModeler` quantifies uptake and storage. Wildfire: `WildfireRiskAnalyzer` and `FireRiskAssessor` assess hazard, supported by the data types `FireIncident`, `FireWeatherObservation`, and the enumerations `FuelType` and `FireDangerRating`. This split mirrors how forestry science itself partitions the problem — state assessment, carbon accounting, and disturbance risk are separate modeling lines over shared geographic data.

Evidence: `tests/unit/` contains six files with 68 test functions; `tests/integration/` adds two files with 8 more — 76 tests total. Examples and documentation directories demonstrate inventory-to-risk workflows. Within the monorepo, FOREST showcases domain depth: eleven public names with enumerated vocabularies (`FuelType`, `FireDangerRating`) show how a GEO-INFER module encodes domain typologies directly in its API, making domain reasoning type-checkable rather than implicit in floating-point outputs.
