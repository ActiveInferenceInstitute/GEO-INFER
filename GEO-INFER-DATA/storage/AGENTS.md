# Agent
: storage

## Scope
 This agent is responsible for handling operations within the `storage` scope.

## Capabilities
 Core capabilities include: - Management of **filesystem** processes - Management of **minio_storage** processes - Management of **optimizer** processes - Management of **postgresql** processes - Management of **redis_storage** processes

## Integration

- **Location**: `GEO-INFER-DATA/storage`
- **Type**: Directory Node
